## What you'll get

Each morning a Claude Code routine reads a set of security news feeds, CISA's catalog of exploited vulnerabilities, and the web, then writes one issue with four parts:

1. **Top stories**: the three to five things worth knowing, each with why it matters and links to the sources.
2. **Market**: funding, acquisitions, and other moves that show where the industry is heading.
3. **Today's concept**: one item from the [learning track](../track.html), covering how the attack works and how defenders prevent and detect it.
4. **Hands-on**: one lab on a legal practice platform that exercises the concept.

Sundays replace the concept with a review of the week and a short quiz.

## Steering it

- To cover something sooner, add a line to `digest/requests.md`.
- To change the order or the topics, edit `digest/curriculum.md`.
- To change tone, length, or sections, edit `digest/INSTRUCTIONS.md`.

Every claim links to its source. Treat this as a reading list with commentary, and check anything you act on against the original.
